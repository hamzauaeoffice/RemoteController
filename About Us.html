About UniReto

iOS · Universal Remote Control

UniReto turns your phone or tablet into a universal remote control for compatible Smart TVs and connected entertainment devices. Instead of juggling multiple remotes — or hunting for a lost one — UniReto lets you discover, connect to, and control supported devices on your local network from a single app.

What UniReto Does

UniReto scans your local network to find compatible TVs and connected devices, and helps you pair with them so you can send remote-control commands directly from your device. Once connected, UniReto is designed to support the everyday controls people expect from a remote, including:

  • Power on/off
  • Volume up/down and mute
  • Channel and menu navigation
  • D-pad and touchpad-style navigation
  • Playback controls (play/pause, previous/next, rewind/fast-forward)
  • A number pad for direct entry
  • Voice-control input, where supported by the connected device
  • Quick shortcuts to TV functions or apps
  • Screen mirroring/casting, where supported
  • A sleep timer
  • Management of previously connected devices, with the ability to rescan and discover new ones

Why We Built It

Modern living rooms often have multiple devices, and physical remotes get lost, run out of batteries, or simply don't support everything a smart TV can do. UniReto's goal is to bring these remote-control functions into one convenient, easy-to-use app on the device you already carry with you.

Compatibility

UniReto is designed to work with a range of compatible Smart TVs and connected devices, but not every feature is guaranteed to work with every TV or device. Compatibility can depend on factors outside of our control, including the device manufacturer, model, firmware or operating system version, your local network configuration, the communication protocols the device supports, and the permissions granted to UniReto on your phone or tablet. We recommend checking that your device is on the same network as your phone or tablet and that it supports the relevant remote-control protocols before assuming a feature will work.

Premium Features

UniReto may offer optional premium features available through an in-app purchase or subscription. Purchases are handled through the Apple App Store, and previously purchased premium access can be restored from within the app.
